Put your sound file in this folder and name it:

    bloodmoon.ogg   (best)
    bloodmoon.wav
    bloodmoon.mp3

Same for spawn, morning, night and survived (.ogg, .wav or .mp3).
survived plays at the dawn after a blood moon, instead of the morning bell that day.

Want variety? Add more: bloodmoon2.ogg, bloodmoon-scary.wav, ... (any name that starts
with bloodmoon, spawn, morning, night or survived). One is picked at random each time.

Then start the game. In the F1 console, type  bmsound test  to hear it right away,
or press Insert in game to open the settings window (volumes, on/off, Test buttons).
