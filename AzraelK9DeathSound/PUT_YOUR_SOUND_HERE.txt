Optional: put your own death sound in this folder, named:

  k9death.ogg   (or k9death.wav / k9death.mp3)

With it, you hear your clip when a wolf, dire wolf, coyote or zombie dog dies.
Without it (or without this mod on your PC), you hear the bicycle horn like everyone else.

Your clip only plays on your PC. Other players hear the horn unless they add their own clip.
